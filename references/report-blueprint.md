# Report blueprint

Follow the user's supplied report template field order. The two blueprints below are routing guides, not replacements for the current template. When no target template is supplied, use the full fallback and formatting rules in [template-fidelity.md](template-fidelity.md).

Unless the user expressly requests another numbering system, render headings as `一、大标题` → `1.中标题` → `1）小标题` using real Word heading styles.

## Small-enterprise report

1. `一、授信方案`：客户、额度、品种、期限、用途、还款、担保、历史变化、准入。
2. `二、客户基本情况`：尽调综述、工商及变更、股权与实控人、经营、关联企业、负面信息、我行合作。
3. `三、关键数据核查`：税务、开票、收入及流水、结算、人行资金流、负债及征信。
4. `四、财务分析`：报表口径和质量、重大科目及前五大明细、盈利、偿债、现金流、财务综述。
5. `五、担保情况`：保证人/担保公司/抵质押物、代偿能力、独立性、处置或追索价值。
6. `六、授信结论`：流动资金需求、合理性、还款来源、压力情景、风险与缓释、叙做理由。

The operating section of the supplied small-enterprise format is not a brief profile. Preserve its question/answer structure where present and normally cover: what products/services are provided and their use scenarios; product-by-product functions/models/applications; business and profit model; product revenue and margin structure; customer and supplier tables; settlement and fund occupation; business growth; industry; named competitors; and core competitiveness. Insert the relevant business-model diagram at the operating-model discussion.

## Complex-structure report

1. `一、业务方案`：授信主体、集团、品种、用途、来源、历史和准入。
2. `二、尽调综述`：背景、现场、财务、第三方、担保和交叉验证。
3. `三、客户情况`：治理、集团边界及控制、业务板块/产品、客户供应商、行业竞争、合规。
4. `四、财报分析`：申请人本部、合并及关键集团成员；科目、盈利、现金流和情景测算。
5. `五、担保情况`：集团成员、资金归集、关联交易、交叉担保、或有负债、保证合理性。
6. `六、授信风险及叙做理由`：风险按来源拆分，量化缓释和还款覆盖。

The operating section of the supplied complex-structure format normally requires: main business; product/model parameter and price-band comparison; revenue by product/region/channel; operating-model diagram; procurement, production/outsourcing and sales modes; capacity or key facilities; top customers and suppliers with terms; price and cost structure; domestic/overseas industry size and drivers when relevant; competitive landscape; named peer comparison; and applicant-specific competitive advantages. Do not omit these items merely because a shorter generic corporate profile would fit the field.

For both formats, follow the product coverage gate in [business-analysis.md](business-analysis.md). Tables should be followed by causal analysis, not number repetition; diagrams should be followed by fund-occupation analysis.

## Decision-ready summary order

Use this order in the opening overview:

1. Who the applicant is and why the relationship exists.
2. What facility is requested and why the amount matches the operating cycle.
3. What business generates repayment and what independently verified it.
4. What the key risk is and how the structure/guarantee/liquidity contains it.
5. The proposed credit conclusion.

## Tables expected when relevant

- Facility comparison: prior / current / change / reason
- Product revenue and gross margin by period
- Top-five customers and suppliers with share and terms
- Industry/competitor comparison tied to the applicant's products
- Revenue/tax/invoice/bank-flow reconciliation
- Debt maturity and guarantee schedule
- Material-account movement and top-five detail
- AR/AP aging and subsequent settlement
- Inventory category/age/turnover and realizability
- Downside cash and debt-service bridge
