# Materials checklist

Use this checklist to inventory evidence and identify only gaps that affect the credit decision.

## Facility and parties

- Applicant legal name and unified social credit code
- Requested amount, currency, product, term, purpose, drawdown and repayment method
- Existing bank approval/use and requested change
- Guarantee, collateral, guarantor, valuation, ownership and encumbrance
- Group boundary, related parties and entity scope of each dataset

## Corporate and management

- Business licence, articles, capital verification or paid-in proof
- Share register, ownership chart, historic changes, actual-controller basis
- Controller/key-management résumé, assets, liabilities and credit reports
- Qualifications, licences, patents, trademarks, headcount and social insurance
- Premises, production/warehouse evidence and site-visit record

## Operations

- Product catalogue, price list, BOM or service scope, delivery/acceptance process
- Revenue split by product, geography, channel and entity for comparable periods
- Top customers and suppliers with amount, share, start year, terms and evidence
- Contracts, orders, invoices, delivery notes, acceptance records and payment proof
- Capacity, output, sales volume, inventory, procurement and sales forecast
- Industry data, competitor list and comparable operating/financial metrics

## Finance and verification

- Three annual periods plus latest interim financial statements, preferably audited
- Audit reports, notes, consolidation scope and accounting policies
- Full trial balance and subledgers for all material accounts
- AR/AP aging, inventory detail and aging, fixed-assets register, loan schedule
- All-bank statements covering the verification period, with account list
- VAT and corporate-income-tax returns, invoice ledger and tax payment proof
- Credit reports, bank facility contracts, guarantees, leases and contingencies
- Related-party transaction and balance detail; restricted or pledged assets

## Minimum drafting gate

Do not finalize until the applicant, amount, facility, purpose, repayment source, guarantee, reporting periods, operating model, current debt, revenue verification basis, and material financial-account explanations are determined. If a non-material attachment is unavailable, omit unsupported detail rather than inserting a placeholder into the report.
