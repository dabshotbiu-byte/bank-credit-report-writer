# Template fidelity and fallback structure

Read this reference before drafting any full report. It distills the four supplied reports into reusable rules and deliberately excludes their customer names, amounts and other case facts.

## Authority order

1. The user's current instructions override every sample.
2. A supplied target report controls field order, mandatory questions, tables and figure placement.
3. Historical reports control voice, analytical density and the expected level of detail, but never supply facts for a new applicant.
4. When no target report is supplied, use the matching fallback below. Do not replace it with a generic company profile or consultancy-style report.

Read the complete target template before drafting, including prompts, tables, appendices, captions and repeated conclusion fields. Separate template instructions from case facts. Build a field map that records, for every template field: evidence needed, intended conclusion, table/figure requirement and source location. Complete every field or remove an inapplicable optional field cleanly; never leave an instruction, blank prompt, placeholder or request for more information in the report.

## Visible heading ladder

Unless the user expressly requests a different scheme, use exactly three visible levels:

- Level 1: `一、大标题`
- Level 2: `1.中标题`
- Level 3: `1）小标题`

Continue the numbering independently within each parent section. Do not mix `1.1`, `（一）`, `1、`, Markdown `#` headings or unnumbered bold labels into the same report. Implement the ladder with real Word Heading 1/2/3 styles and numbering definitions; the visible marker must appear in the rendered document and the heading levels must remain available for navigation.

## Fallback: small-enterprise report

Use this order when no target template is provided:

1. `一、授信方案`
   - `1.授信方案及用途`
   - `2.叙做理由`
   - `3.政策准入及合规判断`
2. `二、客户基本情况`
   - `1.尽调综述`
   - `2.企业概况及发展历程`
   - `3.股权结构、实际控制人及管理团队`
   - `4.主营业务及所属行业情况`
   - `5.收入结构、主要客户及供应商`
   - `6.关联企业、涉诉及负面信息`
3. `三、关键数据核查`
   - `1.纳税及开票核查`
   - `2.银行流水及真实销售回款核查`
   - `3.征信、负债及结算核查`
4. `四、财务分析`
   - `1.财务报表及数据质量`
   - `2.重大科目及前五大明细`
   - `3.盈利、周转、现金流及偿债能力`
   - `4.财务综述`
5. `五、担保情况`
6. `六、授信结论`
   - `1.授信合理性及还款来源`
   - `2.风险点及缓释措施`
   - `3.授信结论`

Where the working template uses Q1/Q2/Q3 prompts, retain those questions as field labels but answer them with the same three-level internal hierarchy and a finished narrative.

## Fallback: complex-structure report

Use this order when no target template is provided:

1. `一、业务方案`
2. `二、尽调综述`
3. `三、客户情况`
   - governance and look-through ownership;
   - group boundary, related parties and fund management;
   - business, products, revenue structure and operating model;
   - customers, suppliers, capacity/facilities and working-capital cycle;
   - industry, named competitors, competitive advantages and compliance.
4. `四、财报分析`
   - applicant, consolidated group and material members as applicable;
   - data quality, major accounts and top-five details;
   - profitability, cash flow, leverage, maturity and downside scenario.
5. `五、担保情况`
6. `六、授信风险及叙做理由`

## Content balance learned from the samples

The supplied reports devote most substantive space to operations/industry and financial verification rather than background narration. Use these as planning guardrails, not quotas:

- Small-enterprise report: detailed operations and industry normally occupy 25–40% of substantive prose; key-data verification and financial analysis together 30–45%; governance, facility, guarantee and conclusion share the remainder.
- Complex-structure report: customer/business/industry analysis normally occupies 20–35%; financial analysis 40–55%; facility/overview 5–12%; guarantee, risk and conclusion 10–20%.
- A material product may require several pages of scenario explanation, product images or parameter tables, operating-flow analysis and competitor comparison. Do not shorten it merely to balance page counts.
- A table or image is followed by interpretation. A page of figures without a conclusion, or a conclusion without figures, is incomplete.

## Paragraph and section rhythm

Use the recurring pattern found across the samples:

1. Start the subsection with the conclusion or the operating fact that matters.
2. Give the period, amount/ratio, product/model, counterparty and transaction term.
3. Explain the business reason or event that produced the number.
4. Trace the effect to revenue quality, margin, receivables, inventory, cash conversion, leverage or repayment.
5. End with the relationship manager's credit judgment.

Product sections first explain the product in plain language, then show images/parameters where useful, compare models and competitors, and finish with the commercial and credit implication. Financial sections first reconcile the numbers, then analyze material accounts and top-five details, and finish with profitability, cash generation and debt-service capacity. Risks are specific and paired with an existing mitigant, structural control or facility condition.

## Template fidelity gate

Before delivery, compare the rendered report with the field map and confirm:

- all required headings/questions/tables/figures are present and in the expected order;
- the visible heading ladder is `一、` → `1.` → `1）` throughout;
- all editable report text is 楷体 and diagrams use the KaiTi/楷体 font stack;
- the operating and financial sections have comparable depth to the relevant sample archetype;
- no raw template instruction, empty field, TODO, source-narration phrase or unfinished wording remains;
- every material conclusion follows fact → reason → cash-flow effect → credit implication.
