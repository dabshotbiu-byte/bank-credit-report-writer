# Template-derived writing style

This guide abstracts the four supplied reports. It does not preserve their customer-specific facts.

## Voice

- Use a decisive first-person institutional voice: “经营团队认为/经经营团队核实/我行拟…”.
- Refer to the borrower as “申请人” or a defined short name.
- Put the conclusion first, followed by evidence and operating explanation.
- Prefer concrete causal chains: event → account/product effect → cash-flow effect → credit implication.
- Use short numbered reasons for the facility conclusion and specific subsections for detailed analysis.

## Evidence density

A useful paragraph normally contains a period, amount or ratio, named counterparty/product, transaction term, and the analyst's interpretation. Compare at least two periods when explaining change. Tie forecasts to orders, installed base, capacity, contracts, seasonality, price and margin assumptions.

## Preferred constructions

- “申请人2024年及2025年分别实现……，同比……，主要受……影响。”
- “从资金占用看，上游……、下游……，因此流动资金主要沉淀于……。”
- “前五大客户合计占比……，其中……；集中度较高，但……构成回款基础/放大单一客户风险。”
- “在……情景下，预计期末货币资金为……，扣除一年内到期刚性债务后为……。”
- “本次授信主要叙做理由如下：1）……；2）……；3）……。”

## Avoid

- “材料显示”“据称”“大概”“或许”“需核实”“后续关注”“待补充”“相关情况不详”。
- Unsupported promotional adjectives: “领先”“唯一”“顶尖”“前景广阔”. Attribute and date third-party rankings.
- A table followed by prose that merely repeats every number.
- Treating a good customer name as proof of timely payment without terms and collection evidence.
- Treating high growth as automatically favorable without margin, inventory, receivable and cash analysis.
- Hiding risks. State them directly and explain verified mitigation or facility controls.

## Completeness rule

If evidence is insufficient, pause before final drafting and obtain the fact or narrow the assertion. The polished final report must not sound unfinished, but polish never justifies fabricating certainty.
